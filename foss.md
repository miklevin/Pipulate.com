# FOSS SEO Software

### Urgent for Content Creators

Web AI-crawlers are working your content into their training models and not
citing or compensating you. The process whitewashes your content to make it
arguably unrecognizable as yours, with no compensation or acknowledgement.
These companies have billion-dollar warchests to fight you on the legal issues,
so you have to do everything you can to protect yourself and your content.
Explicitly stating the terms under which machine learning systems are allowed
to use your content in their training models is a necessary first step. Use the
<a href="/MLGL">Machine Learning General License, Alpha</a> or something like
it.

### What is FOSS SEO Software?

FOSS SEO Software is free and open source search engine optimization software.
This instance is a compilation of software components which you customize and
assemble into products you can call your own for the purpose of building
audience to your website or other Internet properties. Other FOSS SEO Software
products may be pre-built into one of the deliverable-types you can make here.

#### Who is FOSS SEO Software For?

While labeled "SEO" most of the techniques outlined here are equally applicable
for the fields of ***Data Science*** and ***Data Engineering***.

## What is FOSS?

FOSS stands for "Free and Open Source Software." Free and open source software
is software that is freely available for anyone to use, modify, and distribute.
The source code of the software is also typically made available, which allows
users to study, modify, and improve upon the software.

One of the key principles of free and open source software is that users have
the freedom to use the software for any purpose, to study how the software
works, to modify and improve the software, and to distribute copies of the
software to others. This is in contrast to proprietary software, which is
typically distributed under more restrictive terms and is not made available
for users to modify or distribute.

There are many benefits to using free and open source software, including that
it is generally more transparent and secure than proprietary software, it can
be customized to meet specific needs, and it promotes collaboration and
innovation within the software development community.
